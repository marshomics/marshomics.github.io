We're always interested in hearing from people who want to work on the molecular biology and evolution of microbial conflict — particularly the strange, understudied corner occupied by archaeal defense systems and the viruses that target them.

## What we look for

We're a mixed computational/experimental lab, and we expect everyone to do some of both — though most projects lean one way. The strongest candidates we've worked with share a few things:

- **Genuine curiosity about the biology**, not just the techniques. The defense-system field moves fast and the questions worth asking change every year.
- **Comfort with discomfort.** Half the work is reading messy comparative-genomics output and deciding what's signal. If that sounds fun rather than tedious, you're in good company.
- **Willingness to write.** We expect first-author drafts from PhD students by year three, and we read each other's manuscripts a lot.

You don't need to have worked on archaea before. Most of us hadn't.
